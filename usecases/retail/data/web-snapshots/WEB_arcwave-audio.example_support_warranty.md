Arcwave headphones and earbuds include a 12-month limited warranty from the date of purchase covering manufacturing defects including charging faults.

- Start a claim at support.arcwave-audio.example/claim with your proof of purchase and serial/lot number.
- Arcwave sends a prepaid label; replacement ships within 5 business days of receipt.
- Advance replacement is available for a refundable deposit.
- Damage from liquid or drops is not covered.
