# Nimbus Home - Air Purifier X2 Voluntary Recall (web snapshot)

Nimbus Home is voluntarily recalling Air Purifier X2 units with lot codes NX2-26A to NX2-26F. Lot code is printed on the label under the filter door.

- Stop using the product and unplug it.
- Contact your retailer or Nimbus Home for a free refund or replacement.
- As of 2026-11-12, 41 reports of overheating, 3 minor burns; no fires reported.
